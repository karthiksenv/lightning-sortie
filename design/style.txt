Coastal aviation: procedural three-dimensional arcade flight with crisp faceted silhouettes, carefully proportioned machinery, and a clean editorial interface. Blue-grey city towers and muted green terrain meet a deep teal sea; the hero is satin grey with a smoked gold canopy and luminous orange exhaust. Amber is reserved for mission guidance and active controls, coral for threats, and cool white for instruments. Soft atmospheric daylight, warm dusk, and illuminated nights support a readable third-person chase perspective.
